Commercial Property Coverage Form

Policy Form: CP-2026
Policy Number: POL-2026-001
Line of Business: Commercial Property
State: Illinois

PROPERTY COVERAGE

Coverage applies to direct physical loss of or damage to Covered Property caused by a Covered Cause of Loss, subject to the terms, conditions, limitations, exclusions, and deductibles of this policy.

Covered Property includes buildings and structures described in the Declarations and business personal property located within those buildings.

COVERED CAUSES OF LOSS

This policy provides coverage for direct physical loss or damage caused by covered causes of loss unless specifically excluded or limited elsewhere in this policy or by endorsement.

WATER DAMAGE

Direct physical loss caused by the sudden and accidental discharge or leakage of water or steam from a plumbing system located on the described premises may be covered, subject to applicable exclusions, conditions, limitations, and deductibles.

LOSS CAUSED BY FREEZING

Loss or damage caused by the freezing of plumbing, heating, or air-conditioning systems may be covered only when the insured has exercised reasonable care to maintain heat in the building or has shut off the water supply and drained the systems when the building was unoccupied.

VACANCY

Additional limitations may apply when a covered building has been vacant for the period specified by the applicable policy provisions or endorsement.

DEDUCTIBLE

The deductible shown in the Declarations applies to each covered loss unless otherwise provided by endorsement.

GENERAL CONDITIONS

The insured must promptly notify the insurer of a loss, protect the property from further damage, preserve damaged property for inspection when reasonably possible, and provide information reasonably requested during the investigation of the claim.
