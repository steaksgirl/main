Line of Business: Commercial Property
State: Illinois

FREEZING OF PLUMBING SYSTEMS

This endorsement modifies coverage provided under the Commercial Property Coverage Form.

Coverage for loss or damage caused by the freezing of plumbing, heating, or air-conditioning systems applies only when the insured has exercised reasonable care to maintain heat in the building.

When a building is unoccupied, the insured must either:

1. Maintain heat at a temperature sufficient to prevent freezing; or

2. Shut off the water supply and drain the plumbing and other water-containing systems.

If the insured fails to take reasonable precautions described above, loss or damage caused directly or indirectly by freezing may be excluded.

INVESTIGATION REQUIREMENTS

When a claim involves freezing of a plumbing system, the following facts should be established when reasonably available:

- How long was the building unoccupied?
- Was heat maintained in the building?
- What temperature was maintained?
- Was the water supply shut off?
- Were plumbing systems drained?
- Did the insured inspect the premises during the period of vacancy?
- When was the freezing condition first discovered?

OTHER POLICY PROVISIONS

All other terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations of the Commercial Property Coverage Form remain unchanged except as specifically modified by this endorsement.
