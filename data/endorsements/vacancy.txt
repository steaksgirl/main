Vacancy Limitation Endorsement

Endorsement Form: CP-VAC-2026
Policy Number: POL-2026-001
Line of Business: Commercial Property
State: Illinois

VACANCY CONDITION

This endorsement modifies coverage provided under the Commercial Property Coverage Form.

A building is considered vacant when it does not contain sufficient business personal property to conduct customary operations and is not occupied for customary business purposes.

If the described building has been vacant for more than 30 consecutive days immediately before the date of loss, coverage for certain losses may be limited or excluded as provided by this endorsement.

VACANCY-RELATED LOSSES

When the building has been vacant for more than 30 consecutive days, loss caused by water damage, vandalism, glass breakage, building systems failure, or other causes specifically identified in this endorsement may be subject to the limitations stated below.

For water damage occurring after a building has been vacant for more than 30 consecutive days, coverage applies only when the insured has taken reasonable precautions to prevent damage from freezing, including maintaining heat or shutting off and draining the water system when appropriate.

OTHER POLICY PROVISIONS

All other terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations of the Commercial Property Coverage Form remain unchanged except as specifically modified by this endorsement.
