Water Claim Procedure

For claims involving water damage, the adjuster should establish the source and cause of the water intrusion.

The investigation should determine:

- The source of the water.
- Whether the discharge was sudden and accidental.
- Whether a plumbing, heating, or other building system was involved.
- Whether the building was occupied or vacant at the time of loss.
- The length of any vacancy period.
- Whether heat was maintained during vacancy.
- Whether the water supply was shut off.
- Whether plumbing systems were drained.
- Whether a flood, surface water, sewer backup, or water-backup provision applies.
- Whether any applicable endorsement modifies the underlying policy.

The adjuster should review the applicable policy, endorsements, and declarations before making a coverage determination.

Coverage decisions should be based on the applicable policy language and the facts established during the investigation.
